Before you order

Anything else? WhatsApp us. A real person replies.

What makes these different from regular dog treats?

Each one is a single named ingredient, such as chicken breast or goat lung, slow-dehydrated with nothing added. Many shop treats are biscuits made with flour, sugar, colours or preservatives.

Which dog treats are best for puppies?

Soft-snapping treats such as Chicken Jerky, Chicken Bites and Goat Lungs, broken into small pieces. Skip hard chews and bones for puppies. See our puppy treats page.

How many treats can I give a day?

Each pouch has a serving note. As a rule, keep all treats under about 10% of your dog’s daily food.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. Orders are dispatched in 1–2 working days and delivery is free on full packs across India. Cash on delivery is available on most PIN codes (₹40 fee); paying online saves ₹70 compared with cash on delivery.

Are your treats made in India?

Yes. Everything is made in small batches in Kalyan, Maharashtra.
