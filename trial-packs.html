Why 30 g?

It's enough for 6–10 small rewards, the right amount to know if your pet loves a treat before you buy the bigger pack.

Introducing a new treat

Offer one new protein at a time in small pieces and wait 24 hours before adding the next. Always keep fresh water nearby.

Chews need supervision

Feet, necks, wings, ears and bones are natural chews. Pick a size that suits your pet and stay with them while they chew.

Made in small batches

Every pack is one ingredient, slow-dehydrated in Kalyan, Maharashtra, with no preservatives, grains or fillers.
