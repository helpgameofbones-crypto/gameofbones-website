7. Safe feeding

Treat responsibly.

  • Our products are treats and supplements to a complete and balanced diet, not a replacement for it. They are not medicines and are not a substitute for veterinary advice.
  • Read the pack and product page before feeding. Introduce any new treat gradually, choose a size suited to your pet, keep fresh water available and always supervise your pet while it eats, especially with chews and bones.
  • If your pet has allergies, a medical condition or a special diet, or shows any unusual reaction, stop feeding and consult your veterinarian.
  • Store packs as shown on the pack and use them within the stated period. Do not feed from a pack that is damaged, mouldy or smells unusual. Contact us instead.
