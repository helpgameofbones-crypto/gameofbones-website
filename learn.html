The uncomplicated treat guide

Clear answers for curious dog parents.

The live guide covers choosing, serving and storing dehydrated treats. It is general information, not a substitute for advice from your veterinarian.

What does “single ingredient” mean here?

It means each product contains exactly the named meat or fish. The live range has no grains, corn, soy, artificial flavours or preservatives. Always read the current pouch label before serving.

How should I introduce a new treat?

Start with a small amount, observe for 24 hours, and introduce one protein at a time where possible. Keep fresh water available and retain your dog’s usual balanced food routine.

How much should I give?

Treat needs vary by dog. The store advises that treats should make up no more than 10% of daily calories. Consider size, usual food and activity level; treats do not replace a complete and balanced diet.

Are chews suitable for every dog?

Chews need active supervision. Choose an appropriate size and style for your dog, remove small or unsafe pieces, and consult a veterinarian if you have questions about a specific dog’s needs.

How should I store a pouch?

Jerky and bites generally keep 4–6 weeks at room temperature in a sealed pouch. Refrigerate fish and organ treats after opening and use within 2–3 weeks. Check the pouch for product-specific instructions.

My dog has a health condition—what should I do?

Please speak with your veterinarian before introducing a new treat if your dog has a medical condition, dietary restriction or history of adverse food reactions. For suspected food sensitivity, a vet can guide any elimination diet safely.
