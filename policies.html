Shipping policy

Tracking and delivery questions

Once dispatched, tracking is shared by SMS and/or WhatsApp. If there is a delivery issue, contact us with the order number so the team can help.

Processing time

Orders are normally processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation, Monday–Saturday excluding public holidays. During a sale or festival period, dispatch can take up to 3 business days; we will contact you if there is an unexpected delay.

Tracking, COD and address changes

Tracking is sent after dispatch. COD is available only for eligible PIN codes and may include the displayed handling fee. Address changes or cancellations must be requested before dispatch; once an AWB is issued, the carrier may be unable to change the delivery address.

Returns policy

If something is wrong, we’ll help.

Eligible order issues

For a wrong product, missing item, or transit-damaged sealed pouch, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos. Eligible cases may receive a replacement or refund after review.

Food-safety limits

Because treats are food products, opened or partially consumed pouches and change-of-mind requests are generally not returnable. This does not limit your ability to contact us about a genuine quality or delivery concern.

Eligibility

Eligible for review

  • Wrong product delivered.
  • Sealed pouch damaged in transit; report with photos within 48 hours.
  • Missing item from the shipment.
  • Tracking shows delivered but the parcel was not received; report within 7 days.

Generally not returnable

  • Change of mind after delivery.
  • Opened or partly consumed food products.
  • Quality issues caused by incorrect storage after delivery.
  • Transit damage reported after the 48-hour window.

How to request a resolution

  1. Message us within the applicable window with the order number and photos.
  2. We review the details and confirm eligibility.
  3. For an eligible case, we arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Refunds and cancellations

Approved prepaid refunds are returned to the original payment method; the provider/bank can take several business days to complete the credit. COD refund details are collected securely after approval. An order can be cancelled before dispatch; once a shipment is dispatched, it cannot normally be cancelled.
