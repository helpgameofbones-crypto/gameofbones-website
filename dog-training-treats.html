What makes a good training treat?

Small, tasty and quick to eat. A treat your dog can finish in a second or two keeps the session moving.

How small should training treats be?

About the size of a pea for small dogs, and a little bigger for large dogs. One strip of Chicken Jerky gives several rewards.

How many training treats can I give?

Count training treats as part of the day’s food. Keep all treats under about 10% of your dog’s daily food.

Which training treat is best for a dog that reacts to chicken?

Buff Jerky or Goat Lungs are different proteins from chicken.
