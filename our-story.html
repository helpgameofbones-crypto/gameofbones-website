Our story

Less decoding.
More delight.

Game of Bones began in Kalyan in 2024 after its dog parent found fillers, preservatives and artificial colours on treat labels they did not trust.

Meet the treats
Made for the moments that make a dog’s day.

Our promise

Food should be easy to understand.

The first batches were slow-dehydrated chicken from a Kalyan butcher and shared with local dog parents. Their reaction created a simple rule: the name on the front should be the only thing inside.

Today the range spans jerky, chews, organs, fish and whole prey. Production is still in Maharashtra, with hand preparation, low-and-slow dehydration and humidity-resistant packaging designed for Indian weather.

What guides us

A good treat is straightforward.

Every product is built around one ingredient, clear sourcing and an honest label. We do not add fillers, binders, colours, salt, sugar or “natural flavour.”
