Before you order

Anything else? WhatsApp us. A real person replies.

Are these treats safe for cats?

They are plain fish with nothing added. Break larger pieces small, introduce one treat at a time, and skip fish if your cat has a fish allergy.

How many treats can my cat have?

A few small pieces a day. Treats should stay under about 10% of your cat’s daily food.

Do the fish have bones?

Whole fish have small, soft bones that turn crunchy when dried. Tuna and Mackerel Fillet are boneless.

Can kittens have them?

Once your kitten is eating solid food, a tiny piece of anchovy or tuna is a good start.
