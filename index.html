How fast is delivery?

Orders are dispatched in 1–2 working days, and delivery is free across India. You get tracking by SMS or WhatsApp.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, on most PIN codes (₹40 handling fee). Paying online is ₹30 cheaper.

Are these okay for puppies?

Soft-snapping treats like Chicken Jerky, Chicken Bites and Goat Lungs, broken into small pieces, suit most puppies. Skip hard chews and bones for puppies.

Are bone chews safe?

No chew is risk-free. Every chew page tells you which dogs it suits and when to take it away. Always supervise, and choose a size your dog can’t swallow whole.

How much should I give?

Each pouch has a feeding guide. As a rule, treats should stay under about 10% of your dog’s daily food.
