Questions

Before you order

Anything else? WhatsApp us. A real person replies.

Are dehydrated bones safe for dogs?

No chew is risk-free. Dehydrated bones crunch rather than splinter like cooked bones, but pieces can still break off. Pick a size your dog can’t swallow whole, supervise the whole time, and skip bone chews for dogs that gulp.

Which chew lasts the longest?

Goat Trotter is our densest chew. Goat Trachea and Chicken Neck are medium-length chews. How long any chew lasts depends on your dog.

Which chew is best for small dogs?

Goat Ear, Chicken Wing Tips and Goat Trachea. Avoid hard chews like Goat Trotter for small dogs.

Can puppies have chews?

Skip bones and hard chews for young puppies. Soft treats such as Chicken Jerky or Goat Lungs, broken small, are a better fit.

Do chews clean teeth?

We don’t claim that. Chewing isn’t a replacement for brushing or vet dental care.
